Pre-Turnover / Property Inspection

Practical visual inspection support for property owners and clients.

Inspection Coverage

  • Visible wall and ceiling defects
  • Visible water stains and possible leak areas
  • Flooring and finishing observations
  • Doors and windows
  • Visible cracks and construction defects
  • General workmanship observations
  • Accessible areas requiring attention

Inspection Report

Findings may be documented with photographs and practical observations to help clients identify visible items that may require correction or further professional evaluation.

Important: This inspection is limited to visible and accessible conditions. It is not a structural certification, architectural certification, destructive test, concealed-condition investigation or warranty of hidden defects.
