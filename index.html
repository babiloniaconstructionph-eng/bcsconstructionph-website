PRE-TURNOVER INSPECTION SERVICE

Practical visual inspection support for property owners and clients.

Inspection Coverage

  • Visible wall and ceiling defects
  • Visible water stains and possible leak areas
  • Flooring and finishing observations
  • Doors and windows
  • Visible cracks and construction defects
  • General workmanship observations
  • Accessible areas requiring attention

Service Scope

  • Visual inspection of accessible areas
  • Photo documentation of observed conditions
  • Identification of visible defects and areas of concern
  • Practical observations for property owners and clients
  • Basic punch-list style observations
  • Inspection report or summary where applicable
  • Recommendations for further professional assessment when necessary
Important: This service is a practical visual inspection of accessible and visible conditions. It is not a substitute for structural certification, architectural certification, engineering design, destructive testing, laboratory testing, or inspection of concealed conditions. Where required, appropriate licensed professionals should be engaged for formal engineering or technical certification.
