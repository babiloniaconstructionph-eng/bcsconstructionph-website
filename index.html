Pre-Turnover Inspection

Independent visual inspection support for clients preparing to accept or turn over a completed construction project.

  • Visual inspection of accessible areas
  • Review of visible workmanship and finishes
  • Identification of visible defects and incomplete works
  • Photo documentation of observations
  • Preparation of an inspection report based on visible conditions
  • Useful for residential and commercial property owners

Important: Inspection is limited to visible and accessible conditions. It does not constitute structural certification, architectural certification, destructive testing, warranty of concealed conditions or replacement of the services of a licensed professional where legally required.
