Pre-Turnover / Property Inspection

Practical visual inspection support for property owners and clients who need an additional review before turnover.

Inspection Coverage

  • Visible wall and ceiling defects
  • Visible water stains and possible leak areas
  • Flooring and finishing observations
  • Doors and windows
  • Visible cracks and construction defects
  • General workmanship observations
  • Accessible areas requiring attention

Inspection Report

Findings may be documented with photographs and practical observations to help the client identify visible items that may require correction or further professional evaluation.

Important: This inspection is limited to visible and accessible conditions. It is not a structural certification, architectural certification, destructive test, concealed-condition investigation or warranty of hidden defects. Where required, appropriate licensed professionals should be engaged for specialized certification and design work.
